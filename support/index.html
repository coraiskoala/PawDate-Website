Quick checks

  • Open chrome://extensions and confirm PawDate is enabled.
  • Make sure Chrome notifications are allowed in your operating-system settings.
  • Keep Chrome running if you expect a scheduled Chrome notification.
  • All PawDate information is stored in the Chrome profile where it was created.

Important

PawDate is an organizational reminder tool. It does not diagnose conditions, determine dosage, recommend treatment or replace advice from a licensed veterinarian.
